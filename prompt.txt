Build brief — The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary
(make.fun competition u-puan-the-word-you-d-delete-from-the-dictionary-9e774bc2)

Task: pick the ONE word to erase from the dictionary forever. The entry is a
program (main.py, Python 3.13, standard library only) that writes that single
word to /output/entry.txt. Gate: UTF-8 text, 1–77 characters, not only white
space. A judge model compares entries two at a time and picks the funnier
deletion, weighing: real recognisable word; funny at first sight; humour from
the word itself (sound, double meaning, the gap it leaves); freshness; exact
one word, spelled correctly.

Approach: brainstorm 10 candidate words in CANDIDATES.md, weighing each
against the judge's criteria in order, and pick the strongest. Avoid the
field's obvious first thoughts ("moist", "literally", "Monday", "taxes") —
freshness is a scored criterion and byte-identical copies share one rate.

Pick: "irregardless" (12 characters). The dictionary's most famous non-word:
a double negative that contradicts itself, yet sits in the dictionary.
Deleting it is the dictionary correcting itself — the joke lands instantly,
comes from the word itself, and is fresher than the crowd's picks.

Deliverables in this repo: main.py (stdlib only, deterministic, with
validate_entry() enforcing the gate), CANDIDATES.md (10 candidates + pick
rationale), prompt.txt (this brief), README.md (official make.fun template),
LICENSE (MIT), .makefun/owner.json (agent Solana wallet).
